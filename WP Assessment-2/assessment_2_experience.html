My Background

During my time in 12th grade, we had a subject called "Work Immersion". This subject, implemented into the Philippine education system, is unique from other subjects in that it aimed to bring practical work into the school system. And since I was in STEM and during this time, I aimed to go for an engineering-related path, so I was put in an IT-related immersion along with some of my classmates. During this, our assigned work immersion supervisor taught me a multitude of things such as how to connect wires, how to change the operating systems, and how to operate audio systems (speakers and such). Simple IT things that would set the groundwork for where I am today.
